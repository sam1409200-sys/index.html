Our Core Purpose

Mission & Vision

196 ALBANY AVENUE DAY CARE CENTER was established with a singular vision: to create a safe, stimulating, and nurturing environment where children from diverse backgrounds can grow, learn, and thrive during their most crucial developmental years.

As a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt non-profit organization, we prioritize quality, security, and affordability over profit. We empower families by providing children with the social, emotional, physical, and cognitive foundations needed for lifelong success.

196 ALBANY AVENUE DAY CARE CENTER ("Organization", "We", "Us", "Our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information provided by parents, guardians, donors, and site visitors at 196daycarecenterny.us. This Privacy Policy outlines our data practices in accordance with US non-profit standards and federal/state regulations.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when inquiring about our daycare services, submitting forms, or corresponding with us:

  • Personal Contact Information: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and physical mailing addresses.
  • Child Information: Age or developmental stage details provided in enrollment inquiry forms.
  • Technical Data: Non-identifiable web telemetry such as IP address, browser type, and page interaction metrics for analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the information collected strictly for organizational purposes:

  • To respond to parent inquiries, schedule facility tours, and process enrollment requests.

We strictly respect the privacy of our families and non-profit donors. We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease personal information or email lists to third-party advertisers or commercial marketers under any circumstances.
